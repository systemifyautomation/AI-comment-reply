This Privacy Policy explains how ReplyFlow - Social AI Replies ("ReplyFlow", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Chrome extension.

## What ReplyFlow does

ReplyFlow helps authorised users create AI-assisted drafts for social-media comments. It creates drafts only; it does not publish, send, edit, or delete content on a user's behalf.

## Information we handle

ReplyFlow handles the following information only to provide its reply-drafting feature:

- **Email address.** You enter an email address to verify that you have been granted access and to retrieve the clients available to you.
- **Client selection.** The client you choose is used to select the correct reply context.
- **Comment and page context.** When you actively request a draft, the extension sends the selected comment and relevant page context, which may include a page title, URL, and channel or page identifier.
- **Generated replies.** Generated drafts are saved locally in the extension's Chrome storage so you can view recent drafts.
- **Extension settings.** The enabled/disabled preference and verification state are stored locally so the extension works between browser sessions.

## How we use and share information

We use this information only to verify access, identify the selected client, and generate or display the reply draft you requested.

When you request verification, client information, or a reply, the extension sends the necessary information over HTTPS to the ReplyFlow n8n service at `https://n8n.systemifyautomation.com`. That service uses Supabase to manage authorised access and may send the requested comment context to its configured AI provider to generate the draft.
